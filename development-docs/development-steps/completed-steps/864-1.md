## What was done

- `lib/hosting/server-domain.mjs`: `uncertified(rows)` (required names with no usable certificate, returned by `domainState`) and
  `namesToHeal(state, now)` — the domain on, the root helper present, no certificate run at work, the name not failed within the hour
  (Let's Encrypt: «up to 5 authorization failures per identifier … by one account every hour», letsencrypt.org/docs/rate-limits).
- `scripts/serve.mjs`: `healServerNames()` right after `reapplyServerEdge()` at the start, and at the top of `agentsBackAfter` — the one
  end of every build, good or failed — starting `name-up.mjs` detached (nginx, public DNS, the certificate, the HTTPS check).
- `scripts/dedicated-server/certs.mjs`: public DNS first, HTTPS after (`--dns-fresh`, `--no-check` for `names-ready.mjs` / `name-up.mjs`).
